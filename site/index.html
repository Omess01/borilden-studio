About

I'm Ben Børilden — a Process Engineer at GE HealthCare with a Master's degree in Chemistry specializing in analytical chemistry. My professional background combines analytical thinking, process optimization and regulated GMP/GxP production systems.

I work closely with digital manufacturing systems, PAS-X MES, batch documentation, CAPA and process robustness. Alongside my engineering work, I have a growing passion for backend development, networking and self-hosted infrastructure.

Børilden Studio is where these interests meet: development, infrastructure, photography, creative technology and experimentation. This website itself is entirely self-hosted — frontend, backend and infrastructure.
